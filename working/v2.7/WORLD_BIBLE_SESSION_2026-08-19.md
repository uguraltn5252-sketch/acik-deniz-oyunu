# FOULWAKE v2.7 — TEMPORARY WORLD BIBLE

Status: DRAFT / session visual-audit aid
Canonical baseline: v2.6 STABLE / LOCKED
Active branch: v2.7-design

This file records the story/world constraints used for the current visual gate. It does not add mechanics and does not resolve intentionally ambiguous lore.

## Era / Date
- 1721.

## Geography
- Arden Kingdom.
- Departure context: San Cordelio.
- Destination: Saint Verena Quarantine Port.

## Mission
- Carry the experimental preparation and its formula to Saint Verena so Arden can potentially reproduce it.
- Arrival at the destination does not immediately end danger; Saint Verena uses quarantine anchorage and a final night before health officers board at dawn.

## Political / Plague Context
- Arden is under a new plague wave.
- Queen Eleonora wants to save the population, but the formula also matters to royal power because plague-era trade families have become stronger as the state has become more dependent on them.

## Medicine / Formula
- Doctor Mattias Veyr developed an experimental preparation in San Cordelio.
- Results are promising but uncertain; it must not be visually represented as a guaranteed miracle cure.
- The formula itself is strategically more important than individual medicine crates because it may allow Arden to produce the preparation.

## Siyah Mühür
- A port-side name for an ambiguous network of commercial interests, debt, threats, bribery and sabotage.
- It is not to be visualized as an overt fantasy cult or a confirmed monolithic secret society.
- Black wax may be a clue, coincidence or rumor; illustrations must not turn it into proof.
- Preferred sabotage logic: make disaster look like bad luck, bad seamanship or bad collective decisions. "Accident is cheaper than crime" is the tonal logic.

## Gusto Varela
- The returning captain disappeared in San Cordelio.
- Missing logbook pages, black wax and conflicting witness accounts exist.
- Canonical answer to his fate is intentionally absent.
- Illustration may imply evidence or suspicion but must not show a definitive killer, escape, corpse or confirmed conspiracy answer.

## Known Factions / Actors
- Queen Eleonora / Arden crown.
- Doctor Mattias Veyr and plague medicine context.
- Crew and traitors on the voyage.
- Port trade interests referred to as Siyah Mühür.

## Technology / Material Culture
- Early 18th-century maritime world.
- Sailing ship, period clothing, paper records, wax seals, port bureaucracy, quarantine infrastructure and practical maritime tools.
- Avoid modern hardware, modern medicine visuals, electric lighting, modern firearms styling, industrial steamship language and other anachronisms.

## Humor Rules
- The plague, death, sabotage and drowning remain serious.
- Humor comes from people, bureaucracy, petty priorities and absurd behavior inside a dangerous world.
- Default humor level: 1 (background humor).
- Level 2 main visual jokes are selective, mainly on suitable Power or comic Map cards.
- Never make death itself the joke.

## Repeating Background Motifs
Possible low-frequency background NPC/motifs:
- gull
- rat
- unlucky sailor
- cook
- sailor looking for lemons
- crewman tying a bad knot

These motifs must never imply hidden mechanical information.

## Motifs Not To Overuse
- compass
- anchor
- ship wheel
- rope
- ship silhouette
- stars
- old map graphics

Use only when the specific card needs them.

## Visual Direction
- hand-drawn ink
- limited color
- adult caricature
- slight old-print feel
- maritime grit
- dry humor
- LINE > COLOR > TEXTURE
- approximately 70% line / 20% color / 10% texture-shadow

## Ambiguities That Must Remain Ambiguous
- Gusto's final fate.
- Whether black wax proves Siyah Mühür involvement.
- Whether every rumor about the network is true.
- Whether the experimental medicine will ultimately save Arden.

Core rule: ILLUSTRATION MUST PRESERVE AMBIGUITY.
