## Review gate

These five cards are the first visual gate. Do not propagate the visual language to the full 121-card set until:
1. title scale and flama treatment are approved,
2. rules text is legible at real size,
3. illustration remains strong in line-only form,
4. humor does not obscure mechanics,
5. Power vs Rotten Provisions back indistinguishability is verified,
6. Sea/Rock back does not reveal category.
